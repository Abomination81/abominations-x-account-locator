See X's own account-location label while you scroll

The extension places X's public Account based in disclosure on ordinary and quoted posts. Choose any badge color; Abomination green is the default.

  • Regular and quoted posts
  • Your own account is automatically excluded
  • Custom badge colors
  • Local caching for smoother scrolling
  • USA, N. America, and UK abbreviations

About two minutes

Install in Chrome

  1. 1
    Download the extension. Use the green download button above, then open your Downloads folder.
  2. 2
    Unzip the download. On a Mac, double-click the ZIP. On Windows, right-click it and choose Extract All.
  3. 3
    Open Chrome extensions. Copy chrome://extensions into Chrome's address bar and press Enter.
  4. 4
    Turn on Developer mode. The switch is in the upper-right corner of the Extensions page.
  5. 5
    Click Load unpacked. Select the unzipped Abominations-X-Account-Locator folder—not the ZIP file.
  6. 6
    Open or refresh X. Sign in at x.com and scroll. Location badges appear after X returns its public account-location result.
Keep the unzipped folder. Chrome loads the extension from that location. Moving or deleting it will disable the extension.

Use the popup

Pin the extension from Chrome's puzzle-piece menu, then click its green crosshair icon to:

  • Choose a badge color
  • Pause or resume lookups
  • View lookup and rate-limit status
  • Clear the local cache

Privacy

There is no developer-operated server, advertising, analytics, or data brokerage. Requests needed for the feature go only to X and X's asset domain through your existing X session. Settings and the bounded lookup cache stay in Chrome's local extension storage.

Read the full privacy explanation.

Important context

X says its account-location field is inferred from aggregated IP addresses. It may be inaccurate and is not proof of nationality, identity, physical presence, or who controls an account.

Updating or removing it

Update: Download the latest ZIP, replace the old folder, open chrome://extensions, click Reload on the extension card, and refresh X.

Remove: Open chrome://extensions and click Remove. Chrome also removes the extension's local settings and cache.
